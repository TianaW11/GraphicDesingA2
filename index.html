The ParksPeople 2024 survey is very extensive and presents many results from different perspectives of those who use green spaces, from residents to staff. As you scroll down, I'll share more information on the concerning results that support my page's purpose: to raise awareness and drive change through small actions.
































I chose this UN Goal, specifically 11.7, for personal reasons. I’m very adventurous, and I love nature. I’ve always been drawn to nature in my creative projects, and it’s always been my inspiration. Some days when I feel like walking home, there’s a trail from the subway station to my neighbourhood, and it’s a nice walk through nature where I can clear my mind and just enjoy the natural beauty. It’s such a stark contrast to bustling downtown, and it provides a much-needed break. In a world that feels more artificial by the day, seeing and absorbing the purest, most real parts of the earth is so grounding.
When I discovered these statistics, I think they have the greatest impact on parks and green spaces. That's why I used to represent that nature is dying. 80% is a large percentage of cities that don't have enough funding to preserve their green spaces. When the budget isn't met, staff can't provide enough resources to maintain the spaces. When something breaks or affects residents' use of the space, it affects their experience and can lower the health percentages shown in the video over time. This also affects future development and any approaches to making the spaces even better for residents and animals, such as barrier-free access or building wildlife features like birdhouses and planting native trees. There are many ideas to make parks more enjoyable for everyone, but an insufficient budget will hinder that. That's why we need to make those small changes.



































I love seeing people use parks: kids running around, playing fetch with their dog, friends going on a picnic, the running club hitting their next kilometre, or birdwatchers spotting a blue jay. Green spaces are free, open places to experience life's joys. This is why I’m passionate about preserving nature, and if you are too, I highly recommend supporting charities and organizations within Canada (links provided below).
